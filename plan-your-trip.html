Before you go

The essentials, made simple

A few thoughtful arrangements make it easier to spend your time on the water, in the community and out exploring.

01 / GETTING HERE ↗

Fly into Madang

Madang Airport is served by domestic flights, commonly connecting through Port Moresby. Routes and schedules can change, so confirm current services with your airline before booking onward plans.

02 / WHEN TO VISIT ☼

Come ready for the tropics

Madang is warm and humid throughout the year, with sunshine and rain possible in any season. Check a current forecast, pack light layers and keep a flexible day for boat trips.

03 / GETTING AROUND ⌖

Arrange local transport

Ask your accommodation to arrange airport transfers and trusted local transport. For islands and marine trips, book with local operators and confirm the meeting point, conditions and return time.

04 / WHERE TO STAY ⌂

Choose your base

Madang town is a practical base for services and day trips; waterfront stays offer an easy start to time on the water. Reserve ahead, especially around local events, and check what meals and transfers are included.

Travel well

A few things worth sorting early.

  • ✓

    Check Papua New Guinea entry requirements and travel advice for your nationality before booking.

  • ✓

    Confirm flights, accommodation, airport transfers and any boat trips directly with providers.

  • ✓

    Ask about cash, connectivity, meals and what to bring; keep valuables secure and follow local guidance.

  • ✓

    Be a considerate guest: ask before taking photographs and respect community, cultural and marine-site protocols.
