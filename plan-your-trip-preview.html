Before you go

The essentials, made simple

Madang rewards a slower pace. Plan the important details early and leave a little space for local recommendations along the way.

01 ✈

Getting here

Fly into Madang Airport on a domestic connection, often via Port Moresby. Airline routes and timetables can change, so check current services and allow a buffer between flights.

02 ☼

When to visit

Expect warm, humid weather throughout the year, with sunshine and tropical showers possible in any season. Check the forecast before you travel and keep boat days flexible.

03 ⌖

Getting around

Ask your accommodation to arrange airport transfers and trusted local transport. Book island and marine trips with local operators, and confirm meeting points and return times.

04 ⌂

Where to stay

Madang town is a useful base for services and day trips; waterfront accommodation puts you close to the water. Reserve ahead and confirm meals, transfers and what is included.

Travel well

A few things worth sorting early

Considerate, well-prepared travel makes for a better visit—for you and the communities welcoming you.

✓

Check before you book

Review current Papua New Guinea entry requirements and travel advice for your nationality, and confirm flights and accommodation directly with providers.

✓

Plan for local logistics

Ask about cash, connectivity, meals and transfers. Keep valuables secure, allow extra time between activities and follow local guidance.

✓

Be a thoughtful guest

Ask before taking photographs, respect community protocols and follow local advice around cultural sites and the marine environment.

✓

Pack for the tropics

Bring light clothing, sun protection and rain cover. If diving, confirm equipment, certification requirements and conditions with your operator.
