Who we are

About Discover Madang

A practical, respectful guide to Papua New Guinea’s northern coast — its reefs, islands, living cultures and the communities that make every visit meaningful.

Project insight

Problem, audience and goals

Discover Madang responds to a clear opportunity: travellers interested in diving, islands and culture often struggle to find coherent, trustworthy information about Madang Province in one place.

The problem

Information about Madang is scattered across outdated pages, social posts and operator sites. First-time visitors need clearer answers on how to arrive, what to do, how to travel respectfully and how to contact people on the ground.

Target audience

  • Independent travellers and small groups planning a PNG trip
  • Divers and snorkellers seeking reef and wreck information
  • Visitors interested in culture, villages and responsible tourism
  • People who need practical planning tips before they book

Primary goals

  • Present attractions and experiences in a scannable, filterable way
  • Support trip planning with essentials and sample itineraries
  • Encourage respectful cultural engagement
  • Provide an accessible enquiry path to local contacts

Major user tasks

  • Browse and filter attractions; save favourites
  • Learn about culture, festivals and travel etiquette
  • Plan timing, transport and a short itinerary
  • Send an enquiry with clear validation feedback

Our purpose

Why this site exists

Madang is often described as one of the most beautiful places in the Pacific — and one of the least visited. This site helps travellers find clear, honest information about diving, islands, culture and practical planning so more people can experience the province with care and confidence.

We are not a booking engine. We are a guide: curated attractions, suggested itineraries, cultural context and contact paths so you can connect with local operators and communities on the ground.

How we work

What guides this project

Three principles shape every page and recommendation on Discover Madang.

◈

Respect for place and people

Madang’s strength is its communities and environments. We prioritise accurate cultural context, responsible travel advice and language that treats hosts as partners, not backdrops.

◎

Clear, practical information

Travel to Madang benefits from realistic expectations. We focus on essentials — getting here, getting around, when to go and how to plan — so decisions are easier and less stressful.

✦

Local-first discovery

Wherever possible we point toward local operators, craft makers, village hosts and community-based experiences so benefits stay closer to the people who share their home.

The place

About Madang Province

Madang sits on Papua New Guinea’s north coast, facing the Bismarck Sea. It is known for clear water, coral reefs and wrecks, offshore islands, rainforest and a remarkable density of languages and cultural traditions.

From mountain communities in the highlands to coastal and island villages, daily life still includes fishing, gardening, craft and ceremony. Visitors who arrive with time, curiosity and respect are often welcomed into that rhythm.

This project

Collaborative web design (IS229)

The site demonstrates multi-page semantic HTML5, a shared CSS design system (Flexbox and Grid), responsive layouts, JavaScript interactions (menu, favourites with localStorage, filters, accordion, form validation) and accessibility practices across every page.

Team contribution

Pages and features are divided among group members. Each person is responsible for structure, styling, behaviour, responsive behaviour and testing of their assigned work, with commits recorded under their own GitHub account. See the project README for the current allocation and testing checklist.
