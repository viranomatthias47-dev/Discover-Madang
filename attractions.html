Dive deeper

Diving

01Intermediate

Planet Rock Reef Dive

Take a boat dive to Planet Rock, an offshore volcanic seamount known for its steep coral pinnacle and schooling pelagic fish.

03Boat dives

WWII Wreck Dives

Explore the WWII B-25 Mitchell bomber wreck off Wongat Island (15–22 m) and the FS-172 U.S. Army freight-ship wreck near Cape Croisilles (about 30 m). Map pins show approximate areas, not exact wreck positions; arrange these boat dives with a local operator.

02Beginner

Magic Passage Dive

Dive a channel between Kranket and Paeowai islands, known for coral and schooling fish; currents can vary with the tide.

Island escapes

Islands

02Half-day

Bilbil Island

Known for pottery-making and easygoing coastal charm, Bilbil offers a relaxed island experience.

03Day trip

Kranket Island

Drop into sandy coves, swim in clear waters and enjoy a full day of coastal relaxation.

Stories of the coast

History

06Landmark

Kalibobo Lighthouse

Also known as the Coastwatchers Memorial, this Madang landmark commemorates the coastwatchers of World War II and looks across the harbour.

07Coastal landmark

Machine Gun Beach

A coastal spot on Coronation Drive, known for its World War II military-history connection and as a place to enjoy Madang's waterfront.

Nature and culture

Nature & Culture

05Easy

Balek Sanctuary Walk

Explore the rainforest setting, sulfur spring and limestone cave at Balek Wildlife Sanctuary, about 20 km from Madang on the Madang-Ramu Highway.

06Half-day

Craft Villages

Bilbil and Yabob villages are known for Madang's traditional hand-built pottery, a craft passed down through generations.

07Morning

Baitabag Rain Forest Birdwatching

Explore the Baitabag Rain Forest – Kau Village birding area, listed by eBird in Madang, for lowland rainforest and local birdlife.
